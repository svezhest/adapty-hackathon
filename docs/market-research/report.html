What this means for Adapty

  • Adapty’s AI covers the road to the sale: Paywall Generator, AI Editor, Autopilot, Mail. After the sale, when a charge goes wrong, support works by hand in the Profiles screen.
  • RevenueCat already sells to support teams. Adapty has no answer for them yet.
  • Adapty earns 1% of every renewal. A user calmed down and kept, instead of refunded or charged back, keeps paying both the app and Adapty.
  • Adapty already has the data to fix the 54%: subscription state, cancellation and billing events, access levels. What is missing is the agent that reads it and answers the user. That is what we built.

Method

US App Store top-grossing charts, 8 categories: the top 5 and 5 apps from ranks 41–60 in each, social and streaming platforms and hardware companion apps excluded. 74 apps, up to 300 most recent reviews each, 21,825 reviews, 92% written in 2026, from Apple’s public review feed. Every 1–3★ review (9,963) was classified by Claude Haiku; the 1,617 purchase complaints were then read again for pain, wish, support outcome, escalation, plan and who can fix it. Spot check of 10 random detailed labels: 8 correct. Reviews are public complaints, not ticket volume: most unhappy users write to support or their bank and never post a review.

Platform benchmarks that point the same way: refunds by 2–5% of paying users (Adapty, 11,000+ apps); billing errors behind 32% of Google Play and 15% of App Store cancellations (RevenueCat); RevenueCat for support teams.
