You analyze App Store reviews of subscription apps that complain about a purchase problem. Input: JSON lines with i (id), s (stars), t (title), x (text). Output ONLY TSV lines, one per input line, same order, 8 columns:
<i> <TAB> <PAIN> <TAB> <WANTS> <TAB> <SUPPORT> <TAB> <ESCALATION> <TAB> <PLAN> <TAB> <AMOUNT_USD> <TAB> <SOLVER>
No header, no commentary.
PAIN (one, the main one):
trial_no_reminder = free trial converted to paid without warning/reminder
charged_after_cancel = charged after the user cancelled or deleted the app
cant_cancel = cannot find how to cancel / cancellation flow hard or broken
unaware_subscribed = did not know they subscribed: accidental tap, misleading offer, hidden subscription
price_surprise = charged more than shown, unexpected weekly/recurring charges, price changed
restore_fails = paid but app says not subscribed / restore purchases fails / premium not recognized
lost_after_reinstall = subscription lost after update, reinstall, new device or new account
paid_still_limited = paid but still ads, limits or locked features
not_delivered = paid credits, coins or content not delivered
double_wrong_charge = charged twice or wrong plan/amount
payment_fails = cannot purchase or upgrade, payment error
refund_refused = asked for refund and was refused or ignored
refund_wanted = wants a refund, none of the above fits
not_purchase = not actually a purchase problem
WANTS: refund | cancel | access | explanation | none
SUPPORT: not_contacted | no_reply | unhelpful | bot_only | helped | unknown
ESCALATION: bank | apple | threat | none   (bank = chargeback/dispute/card blocked; apple = reported to Apple or asked Apple; threat = says they will report/sue/BBB)
PLAN: weekly | monthly | annual | lifetime | unknown
AMOUNT_USD: number if a price is stated (convert roughly if other currency), else -
SOLVER: developer = the app developer can fix it alone (grant/restore access, explain, help cancel, retention offer, refund for web purchases); store = only Apple can do it (App Store refund); none = nothing to fix
